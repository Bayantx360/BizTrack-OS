The Problem

Running a business on
guesswork is expensive.

Most Nigerian SMEs lose money not because they don't work hard — but because they can't see what's actually happening.

📓
Notebooks don't add up
Manual records get lost, miscalculated, or simply forgotten. At the end of the day, you don't know what you actually made.
📦
Stockouts surprise you
You find out a product has run out when a customer asks for it — not when you still have time to reorder.
💸
Debts go untracked
"I go balance you tomorrow" — and tomorrow never comes. Credit sales vanish without a record or a reminder.
🧾
No receipt, no proof
Customers dispute amounts. You have no record. Handwritten receipts tear, fade, and get lost in a pile.
📉
Profit is invisible
Sales look good but the money isn't there. You don't know which products actually make money and which drain it.
💰
Expensive tools don't fit
Western software costs dollars, requires training, and wasn't built for how Nigerian business actually works.
