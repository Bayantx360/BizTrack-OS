The Problem

Running a business on guesswork is expensive.

Most Nigerian SMEs lose money not because they don't work hard — but because they can't see what's happening.

📓
Notebooks don't add up
Manual records get lost or miscalculated. At day's end you don't know what you actually made.
📦
Stockouts surprise you
You find out a product has run out when a customer asks — not when you still have time to reorder.
💸
Debts go untracked
"I go balance you tomorrow" — and tomorrow never comes. Credit sales vanish without a record.

No receipt, no proof
Customers dispute amounts. Handwritten receipts tear, fade, and get lost in a pile.

💰
Expensive tools don't fit
Western software costs dollars, requires training, and wasn't built for Nigerian business.
